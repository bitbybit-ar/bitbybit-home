We are a group of people improving every day.

We build healthy habits in our own lives, and we ship tools that help
others do the same. We believe in collaboration over competition, in
free and open software over walled gardens, and in a future where the
infrastructure of everyday life — money, identity, communication — is
not owned by anyone.

We work in public. Our code is on GitHub. Our community is La Crypta.
Our experiments are real products: BitByBit Habits, BitByBit Arena.

Everything we make is small consistent action, stacked over time. Like
sats. Like blocks.

## What we value

- **Action over speculation.** We ship.
- **Collaboration over competition.** We build with the community, not
  against it.
- **Open over closed.** Our code is public. Our decisions are
  documented.
- **Simple over clever.** A boring solution that works beats a clever
  one that breaks.
- **Long-term over short-term.** Stack consistently. Compound.

## What we don't do

- Surveillance, tracking, or anything that monetizes user data.
- Closed ecosystems where users cannot leave with their data.
- Promises we cannot keep.
